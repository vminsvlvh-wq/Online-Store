THE BRAND

Welcome to our shop

Inspired by the chic streets of Paris, Nadia Perfumes are pour femme and pour la vie. Blended and crafted in Europe using intoxicating scents, Nadia is boldly and unapologetically feminine.

The Nadia eau de parfum collection includes citrus, floral, oriental, spicy, oceanic, and woody scents. All fragrances are inspired by natural ingredients, from mandarin to vanilla, rose to vetiver.

The collection includes 100ml bottles for your dressing table and 10ml handbag-sized bottles for travelling. The bottle’s minimal design and simple labelling is timelessly elegant.

Bella is floral and fruity. Noir is spicy, rich, and lingers in every room. Paris Bloom is fresh, aromatic and smoky. There is a scent for every woman and every mood.
